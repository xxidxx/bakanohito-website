Our Story

From Internal Tools to Products

What started as internal tooling for ML teams at Pertamina became a mission to democratize production-grade AI infrastructure.

Q1 2023

Company Founded

Bakanohito Technologies established in Jakarta. Initial focus: internal LLM gateway for Pertamina's Technology Innovation & Implementation division.

Q3 2023

Local AI Gateway v1.0

Released first production version supporting Ollama, llama.cpp, and custom GGUF models with OpenAI-compatible API. Deployed across 3 enterprise teams.

Q1 2024

Prompt Orchestrator Launch

Visual workflow builder for prompt chains with version control, testing, and observability. Adopted by 2 fintech companies for production workloads.

Q3 2024

Eval & Observability Platform

Automated evaluation pipelines with 1,200+ test cases, regression detection, and cost tracking. Reduced hallucination rate by 67% for pilot customers.

2025

Open-Core Transition

Released core gateway and orchestrator under MIT license. Building community around local-first AI development. SOC 2 Type II audit initiated.

Principles

How We Build

Our engineering values shape every product decision.

Local-First

Develop with full fidelity on your machine. No cloud dependency for core workflows. Deploy anywhere when ready.

Standards-Compliant

OpenAI API spec, MCP, OpenTelemetry. Build on open protocols so you're never locked into our ecosystem.

Developer Experience

CLI-first, great docs, typed SDKs, fast feedback loops. Tools that disappear so you can focus on your product.

Security by Default

Data never leaves your VPC. Audit logs, RBAC, SSO (SAML/OIDC), encryption at rest and in transit — included, not upsold.
